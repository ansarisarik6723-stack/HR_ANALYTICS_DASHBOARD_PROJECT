# HR_ANALYTICS & EMPLOYEE ATTRIRTION DASHBOARD | POWER BI
|Power BI | Power Query | DAX |.
Analyzed 1,463 employee records to identify attrition patterns across age, gender, education, salary, department, and job role.

# Key Questions (KPIs)
This dashboard answers critical HR questions:
- What is the overall **attrition rate** in the company?
- Which **age groups** and **education backgrounds** have the highest attrition?
- How does **salary band** impact employee turnover?
- Which **job roles** are most affected by attrition?
- What is the average **tenure, age, and monthly income** of employees?

- # Process
1. **Data Collection**: Employee dataset prepared in Excel.  
2. **Data Cleaning**: Removed duplicates, handled missing values, standardized columns.  
3. **Data Modeling**: Built relationships and created calculated measures using **DAX**.  
4. **Visualization**: Designed interactive charts (bar, pie, line) in **Power BI**.  
5. **Dashboard Development**: Combined KPIs and visuals into a single HR Analytics Dashboard.

# Project Insights
- **Attrition Rate**: 16.2% overall.  
- **Age Factor**: Highest attrition among employees aged **26–35**.  
- **Education**: Employees from **Life Sciences (37.5%)** and **Medical (26.5%)** backgrounds show higher turnover.  
- **Salary Band**: Majority attrition occurs in employees earning **below ₹5,000** monthly.  
- **Job Roles**: **Laboratory Technicians** and **Sales Executives** face the highest attrition.  
- **Tenure**: Employees leaving early (0–2 years) form a significant portion of attrition cases.

  # Conclusion
The HR Analytics Dashboard highlights that attrition is strongly influenced by **salary, job role, and age group**.  
Key recommendations include:
- Improving compensation for lower salary bands.  
- Designing retention programs for high-risk roles (Sales & Lab Technicians).  
- Offering career growth opportunities for younger employees.  

This project demonstrates the power of **Power BI** in transforming raw HR data into meaningful insights for workforce management.
